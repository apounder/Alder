ALDER FOR macOS

Choose AppleSilicon for an M-series Mac, or Intel for an Intel Mac.
This initial build targets macOS 15 (Sequoia) and newer.

Open the DMG and drag Alder into the Applications shortcut.
Eject the disk image, then open Alder from Applications.
Alternatively, double-click the ZIP and drag Alder.app into
Applications. Keep the app bundle intact.

Python, npm, Node.js, a browser, and a localhost server are not required.
The editors, chemistry engine, and renderer are bundled and work offline.
Open files inside Studio or drag them onto its window.

INITIAL TEST BUILDS: these apps have an ad-hoc signature, not an Apple
Developer ID signature or notarization. macOS may block the first launch.
For a copy you trust, try opening it, then use System Settings > Privacy &
Security > Open Anyway and confirm. Managed Macs may not allow this exception.
Do not disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine system-wide.
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

To uninstall, quit Studio and move Alder.app to Trash.
Your calculation files are not part of the app and are left in place.

Startup diagnostics: ~/Library/Logs/Alder/studio.log
Build information and dependency notices: right-click the app, choose
Show Package Contents, then Contents/Resources/ReleaseNotes.
Editor notices are in Contents/Resources/alder/assets/*-LICENSE*.txt.
